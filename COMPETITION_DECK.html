Problem and initial user

Turn label photos into an inventory answer

Proposed user

A small stockroom or campus equipment-audit team with package-label photos and an expected inventory list.

Task: identify duplicates, absent expected IDs, unexpected IDs and photos that need another look.

Workflow to validate

Local processing when internet access is unavailable; a saved report that teammates can inspect and export.

Next evidence: observe an actual audit and compare the complete workflow with an offline single-phone process.

Current evidence uses generated labels. No customer adoption, time-saving or warehouse-deployment claim is made.
